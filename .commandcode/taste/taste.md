# Taste

## Communication
- Writes and expects replies in Chinese (简体中文). Confidence: 0.75
- Gives very terse, high-level feature briefs (e.g. "登录部分增加游客登录，游客登录不能同步") and expects the agent to explore the codebase, figure out the design, and implement end-to-end rather than asking for a spec. Confidence: 0.6
- Reports bugs as a one-line symptom description (e.g. "点击游客登录不跳转页面"), often with an annotated screenshot, and expects the agent to reproduce and find the root cause itself rather than being told where the bug is. Confidence: 0.55
